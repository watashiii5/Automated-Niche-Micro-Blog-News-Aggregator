Managing complex interactions with large language models often gets messy when relying on standard linear chat threads. ThoughtDAG offers a fresh approach by structuring LLM conversations as directed acyclic graphs.

With this editable context graph, users can branch, prune, and reorganize dialogue nodes visually. This makes navigating non-linear reasoning paths far more efficient.

Key highlights:
- Graph-based conversation structure
- Editable context nodes for fine-tuned prompt control
- Visual management of complex LLM interactions
