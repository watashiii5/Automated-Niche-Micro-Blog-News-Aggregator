Spain has officially instructed network providers to restrict access to the web preservation platform Archive.today, targeting both the main site and its various mirrors.

The decision affects users who rely on digital archives to record online content, inspect historical page revisions, and maintain public web records.

- Primary target: Archive.today
- Extended scope: Associated mirror domains

This action reflects expanding legal and regulatory actions against web archiving infrastructure within European territories.
