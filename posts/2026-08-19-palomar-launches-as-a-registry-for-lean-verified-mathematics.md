Mathematician Terry Tao has shared Palomar, a new public registry created specifically for Lean verified mathematics.

As formal verification gains traction across research and computing, Palomar provides a structured index to help track mathematical proofs written in the Lean language.

What to know:
- Serves as a central repository for Lean-verified mathematical content.
- Highlighted by Terry Tao as part of ongoing formalization efforts.
