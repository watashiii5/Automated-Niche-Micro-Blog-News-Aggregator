GLM is taking a major step forward by deploying its own dedicated inference infrastructure. By building a custom setup, the team is optimizing how its language models are served and scaled.

Developing an in-house stack gives AI creators direct control over their compute pipeline. This approach helps tailor hardware performance specifically to the model's unique architecture.

Key advantages of custom infrastructure include:
- Better optimization for specific workloads
- Greater control over compute costs
- Streamlined model deployment and serving

This shift reflects a broader trend of top AI teams taking full ownership of their underlying hosting and serving layers.
