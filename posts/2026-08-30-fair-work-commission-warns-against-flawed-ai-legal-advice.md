Using AI for legal research can backfire if outputs go unchecked. Australia's Fair Work Commission recently issued a sharp warning after encountering inaccurate legal advice generated by artificial intelligence tools.

While generative models offer quick answers, they can hallucinate regulations or apply wrong legal standards. Relying on automated text without expert validation creates major risks for anyone presenting cases before regulatory bodies.

Quick reminders when using AI tools:
- Always cross-check AI outputs against official source documents.
- Treat unverified AI responses as starting points rather than facts.
