As GPUs demand massive thermal design power, datacenter cooling has become a critical bottleneck. Discovered Materials, a new YC-backed startup, is using frontier AI agents to accelerate the discovery of next-generation semiconductor materials that handle extreme heat.

By evaluating models from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Kimi, the team found that AI can rapidly discover dynamically stable materials in hours—a task that historically took human researchers weeks. Their focus includes solving heat bottlenecks in 3D packaging, thermal interface materials, and chip substrates.

Key highlights of their launch:
- Releasing hundreds of AI-discovered materials for research
- Introducing a benchmark to evaluate AI performance in material science
- Matching thermal performance of long-held chemical trade secrets
