Digital banking provider Revolut has confirmed a customer data breach following an attack involving fraudulent government data requests.

Attackers managed to trick internal systems or staff by submitting fake inquiries designed to look like official law enforcement or government demands. This social engineering tactic allowed unauthorized access to internal customer records.

Quick highlights:
- Malicious actors forged official legal and government requests
- Sensitive customer data was compromised during the incident
- The exploit targets compliance processes used by modern fintechs

This incident underlines how social engineering tactics continue to evolve past traditional phishing, directly targeting regulatory and compliance workflows.
