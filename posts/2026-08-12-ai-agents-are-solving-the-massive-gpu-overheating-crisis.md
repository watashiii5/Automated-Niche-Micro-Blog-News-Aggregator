Modern GPUs face a serious thermal challenge, with chip power demands nearly doubling each generation. Discovered Materials, a Y Combinator startup, is deploying AI agents to find novel semiconductor materials that can better manage extreme heat.

By testing frontier models from Anthropic, OpenAI, and Kimi, the founders discovered that AI can identify stable materials in an eight-hour run—a task that usually takes a researcher weeks. Though physical lab synthesis remains an empirical hurdle, their early tests matched thermal performance guarded by top chemical firms.

The team has released their benchmark alongside hundreds of candidate materials:
- Open research on candidate materials for chips
- Benchmarks measuring AI model performance in science
- Analysis of weird model behaviors during long runs
