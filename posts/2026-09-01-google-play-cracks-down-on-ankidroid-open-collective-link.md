Open-source maintainers face new hurdles as Google Play tightens enforcement on external donation links. The popular flashcard application AnkiDroid recently flagged an issue regarding their project's funding visibility on the storefront.

According to a recent issue tracker update, Google Play no longer permits the app to feature its Open Collective donation link. This decision impacts how the project collects financial support directly from its Android user base.

Key details of the change:
- AnkiDroid cannot link directly to Open Collective within the Google Play version.
- Community members are actively discussing the policy impact on GitHub.
- Open-source funding remains a tricky balance on centralized app stores.
