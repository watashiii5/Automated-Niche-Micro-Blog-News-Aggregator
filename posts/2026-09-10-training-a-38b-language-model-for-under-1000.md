Training custom AI models no longer requires massive enterprise budgets. A new experiment demonstrates how to train a 3.8B parameter language model for less than $1,000 while achieving solid benchmark results.

By capping total spend at $998, the project successfully brought the model up to a 0.384 CORE evaluation score.

Key details from the project:
- Total budget: $998
- Model size: 3.8B parameters
- Benchmark score: 0.384 CORE

This budget-friendly milestone shows how accessible training open-source models is becoming for independent builders and researchers.
