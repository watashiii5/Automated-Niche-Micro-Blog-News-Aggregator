AI coding assistants are rapidly changing how software gets built, but machine-generated logic raises clear questions about final accountability. Even when an assistant generates the implementation details, the resulting codebase remains fundamentally yours.

A recent post on Martian Software delves into this topic as developers weigh the balance between fast automation and long-term responsibility.

Core takeaways from the discussion:
- Machine-generated outputs still belong to the human creator.
- Developers must maintain full oversight over shipped logic.
- Tool reliance does not transfer final accountability.
