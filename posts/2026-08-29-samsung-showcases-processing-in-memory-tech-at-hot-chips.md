Samsung presented updates on its Processing-in-Memory (PIM) technology during a Hot Chips presentation, highlighting new approaches to semiconductor architecture.

By integrating compute functions closer to memory chips, PIM aims to tackle long-standing bandwidth limits and change how systems handle data-intensive tasks.

Key takeaways from the showcase:
- Brings computational units directly onto memory hardware.
- Targets performance bottlenecks caused by data transfer.
- Demonstrates evolving design strategies for next-gen hardware.
