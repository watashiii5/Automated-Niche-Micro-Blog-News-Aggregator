Open-source software on mobile hardware just scored an exciting win. Developers have successfully gotten the main camera working on the Fairphone 6 while running PostmarketOS.

Camera support is often one of the hardest hurdles when porting mainline Linux to modern smartphones. Achieving a functional main camera brings open-source mobile operating systems one step closer to practical daily use.

Key highlights:
* Main camera functionality is now active
* Expanded hardware support for Fairphone 6
* Continued progress for Linux-based mobile systems
