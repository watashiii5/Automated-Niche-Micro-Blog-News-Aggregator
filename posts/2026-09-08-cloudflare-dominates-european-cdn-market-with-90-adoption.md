New insights reveal massive market concentration in Europe's web infrastructure landscape. Among European businesses that utilize a content delivery network, nearly nine out of ten depend on Cloudflare.

This staggering usage rate underscores how dominant a single provider has become for web traffic delivery and security across the continent.

Quick highlights:
- Cloudflare captures almost 90% of CDN-using European firms
- Points to significant reliance on a single infrastructure player
