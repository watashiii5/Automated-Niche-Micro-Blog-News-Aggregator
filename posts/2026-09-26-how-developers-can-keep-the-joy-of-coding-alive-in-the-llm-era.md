The rise of AI language models is changing the daily reality of software engineering. As code generation tools become standard, developers are stepping back to ask an important question: how do we maintain the fun and personal fulfillment of writing code?

A recent discussion on the Haskell forum sparked widespread debate among engineers looking to balance productivity with personal satisfaction. Key themes from the community focus on finding balance:

- Re-centering on core logic and high-level architecture
- Embracing expressive languages that prioritize developer joy
- Using AI to handle routine tasks while keeping creative control

Finding happiness in modern software development means actively choosing how to work alongside these powerful tools.
